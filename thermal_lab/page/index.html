WORKLOAD-AWARE CPU ROUTER

A field name alone does not make a job quantum-suitable. The eligible part may be a molecular electronic-structure or many-body quantum kernel inside a larger biology or physics workflow. Preprocessing and result interpretation remain classical.

Why more GPUs may not solve the scaling problem

For an exact dense state-vector simulation, an n-qubit pure state has 2ⁿ complex amplitudes. At 16 bytes per amplitude, adding one qubit doubles this representation's memory. More GPUs can increase the feasible size, but a fixed-factor hardware increase does not remove exponential scaling.

This is an explanatory memory lower bound for one classical representation, not a GPU-runtime prediction or a requirement to encode a useful quantum result. Tensor networks, approximations and problem structure can change classical cost. Quantum algorithms also have state-preparation, error-correction, measurement and accuracy overhead. Biology and physics do not universally receive exponential speedups.

Read the evidence and limitations
  • The slide catalog anchors 2035. The default 3%/year GPU dynamic-energy reduction is an editable serving/software scenario applied to both fleets; bandwidth, memory and installed peak remain the slide values. Set 0% for a fixed-efficiency comparison. The scenario’s five-year check is 2035–2039; an additional conservative guardrail requires all cumulative spending through each displayed year to remain below $30B. No automatic Moore/Neven trend is used.
  • Default: 500 extra scientific jobs/day for B/C; A has zero scientific demand. Explicit QPU purchases are still installed, powered and charged even when unused. Biology/physics selections borrow the chemistry kernel's numeric calibration as an editable proxy, not a benchmark for those fields.
  • Scientific GPU-hours, system price, accepted QPU throughput and hardware feasibility come from the selected saved team profile. These are modeled estimates, not matched-quality measurements.
  • Jobs are spread over 24 hours. Capacity admission and an average service-time screen do not verify queues, bursts, state preparation or four-hour tail latency. The CPU classification is a deterministic demonstration, not an ML classifier or live hardware dispatcher.
  • Served GPU work, served QPU work and unserved work sum to the same baseline GPU-work equivalent. Scientific jobs/day is a separate, clearer job count. CPU orchestration is not counted as extra completed AI work.
  • Construction, electricity, maintenance, prefill and CPU-router allowances are assumptions/proxies. Constant-price mode holds operating rates fixed. The optional inflation view compounds running costs from 2035; hardware purchases and the $30B nominal budget stay fixed. No financing or resale is included. Capital is paid when purchased, including later QPUs; no retired GPU refund.
  • End-to-end TTFT, local site limits, carbon, failure reserves, detailed LPU/context sizing and the official scorer remain outside this screen. Hybrid success requires lower annual energy and lower annual running costs than the improving GPU-only comparator in every deployed year. Before deployment, equal costs are a reference state, not a claimed benefit. A passing model is not challenge certification.
